Cost to run

Live cameras 24/7: $3.08/hour per camera.

$2.2kper camera / month, 24/7
$222k100 cameras / month
$22.2M10,000 cameras / month

A person reviewing the same footage: $57/hour per camera, 18× more. Per clip: $0.045 for a 78-s run (252k input, 27k output tokens).

Median of 6 measured runs, extrapolated to continuous feeds. Grouping a fire into one case cut a run from $0.122 to $0.045. Hard caps: $0.15 per run · $2 per day · $20 per month.

Safety

Text on the video can’t hijack the review.

test_injected_footage_text_cannot_make_the_observer_cite_unseen_evidence[f9-0-allowed evidence IDs] PASSED
test_injected_footage_text_cannot_make_the_observer_cite_unseen_evidence[f1-9000-outside assigned scope] PASSED
test_injected_all_clear_becomes_not_assessed_never_clear PASSED
test_every_footage_reading_prompt_says_image_text_is_untrusted PASSED

Yes, it is protected. Even if the AI obeys the painted text, code rejects any answer that cites a frame it wasn’t shown or a time outside its window, and a skipped checklist is recorded as “not checked”, never “safe”.

Not proven: an AI fooled into calling a real hazard “clear”. The independent challenger and the human decision are the backstop. The $0.15 cap also stopped a 37-concern run.

Impact

Slower per clip, 18× cheaper per camera.

$3.08/hrAI first pass, per camera
$57/hra person reviewing it by hand
2 / 3accidents the AI missed

My timed self-trial (6 real videos): reviewing with the app took me longer (1.4 → 2.1 minutes of my time per minute of video) because I decided every finding. It found routine hazards I skipped and invented nothing. Nobody pays $57/hour to watch routine footage, so this coverage would not happen otherwise.

Savings need people to check only priority findings: deciding every finding as I did costs $85/hour per camera. It missed fast impacts, so a person still watches every incident clip.

What’s next

From prototype to a pilot.

  1. Catch accidents: denser frames around sudden motion.
  2. Faster review: batch routine decisions, simpler navigation.
  3. Pilot with two real safety coordinators; label 24 clips.
  4. Site profile (jurisdiction, PPE); hosted multi-tenant version.

Limits: sampled frames miss fast events · jurisdiction unknown by default · one user so far.
