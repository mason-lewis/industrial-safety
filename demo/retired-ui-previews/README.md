# Sample video previews

These JPEGs are first-frame previews of `demo/videos/clip-001.mp4`, `clip-004.mp4`, and `clip-007.mp4`.
They are derived from Önal and Dandıl's industrial surveillance dataset (2024),
DOI [10.17632/xjmtb22pff.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/xjmtb22pff.1), under CC BY 4.0.
The original source filenames, splits and provenance remain in `demo/manifest.json` and `demo/README.md`.
No safety labels or regulatory applicability are inferred from these previews.
