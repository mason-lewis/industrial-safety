VIDEO CHECK: A visual claim requires actual attached pixels at its cited source timestamps, not only a caption or observer assertion. Each packet lists exactly the claims assigned to you; check all of those IDs. Inspect the whole frame and any cited crop, preserve temporal gaps, occlusion, unclear text and possible scene boundaries. Do not invent audio, identities, causality, medical diagnosis, continuous motion, calibrated distances or speeds. A retained crop has the same timestamp as its parent and adds no temporal coverage.
Saved-review blocks require their frozen original records and linked human decisions, including contradictory or uncertain decisions. Prior chat is not new evidence. General guidance cannot establish a visible event or a saved-record fact. Independently_publishable requires the full qualification to survive. Missing images or unverified visual references are unsupported, never silently approved.
